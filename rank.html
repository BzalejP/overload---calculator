LIFT BREAKDOWN

Estimated one-rep max via the Epley formula, from your best logged set of each lift, divided by your latest logged body weight, mapped to a percentile against the general adult population (not just people who train) using a simplified statistical curve. This is a rough, unisex, for-fun estimate — not a scientific or medical assessment. Body weight is only ever used to normalize the lift numbers, never scored on its own.
